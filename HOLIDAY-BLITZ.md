# OPERATION HOLIDAY BLITZ — August 2026

**The window:** Kenyan schools close for the August holiday in the first week of
August. For four weeks, every parent in the country is asking the same question:
*"what will the children do all holiday?"* — and "holiday tuition" is the
default answer they already budget for. We do not create the demand; we
intercept it.

**The doctrine (Uber-style):** don't market — *blitz*. Free product as the
weapon, referral loops as the engine, geographic density as the strategy,
weekly prize cycles as the drumbeat. Win estates, not "Kenya". Convert the
holiday cohort into school term deals in September.

---

## 1. The Grand Offer (the "free rides")

> ### 🎁 THE WHOLE AI TUTOR. FREE. ALL HOLIDAY.
> Grade 1–12 maths — a personal AI teacher that finds your child's gaps and
> fixes them, with pictures, step-by-step teaching, and a progress report a
> parent can read. **No card. No payment. Gone September? Keep your progress.**

Why this works:
- **It costs us ~nothing** (the AI tutor is self-serve; marginal cost ≈ hosting).
- **It is genuinely the best free maths product in Kenya** — 244-skill G1–12
  graph, diagnostic placement, visual teaching. Eneza is SMS quizzes; we are a
  teacher.
- **"Free for the holiday"** has urgency built in. An open-ended free tier does
  not.
- The paid edge stays intact: **live 1-on-1 lessons** with verified tutors —
  at **full price**. No discounts: the AI tutor's diagnostic report is the
  sales agent. A parent who has just seen "your child is missing G6 fractions
  and G7 equations" doesn't need 50% off to book a tutor for exactly those
  topics — they need the report. Discounting spends capital we don't have to
  convince people the free product already convinces.

The ask at every touchpoint is tiny: *"WhatsApp yourself the link, it takes 2
minutes to see your child's level."* The diagnostic IS the hook — parents pay
attention when you show them exactly where their child stands.

## 2. The Referral Engine (the K-factor machine)

Every student gets a **referral code** (tracked in-product — shipped with this
campaign). The loop:

| Actor | Action | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Student A | Shares their result card / invite link on WhatsApp | Every activated friend = 1 entry in the **weekly draw** + climbs the **Ambassador Board** |
| Friend B | Signs up with A's code, **completes the diagnostic** (activation!) | Full free holiday access + their own code |
| Weekly | Top referrer + 2 random-draw winners | **KSh 100 airtime via M-Pesa** + named shout-out on the page and in groups (publicly announced every Friday) |

Rules that matter:
- **Reward activation, not signup.** A referral counts only when the friend
  finishes the diagnostic. No bot farms, and every "referral" is a genuinely
  onboarded student.
- **Prizes are airtime + public glory** — KSh 100 airtime is real to a
  teenager, trivially sent via M-Pesa, and the *named Friday shout-out*
  ("Brian from Umoja — 31 skills this week 🔥") is half the prize. Recognition
  is free and compounds.
- Cost ceiling: 3 prizes/week × KSh 100 × 4 weeks = **KSh 1,200 total**. If
  even that pinches, week 1 can run on recognition alone — add airtime once
  the first lesson revenue lands.

## 3. Density, not reach (the "city playbook")

Uber launched city by city. We launch **estate by estate, school by school** —
Nairobi first. Winning 30% of one estate's parents beats 0.1% of Kenya:
WhatsApp groups tip when several families in the *same group* are talking about
the same thing.

**Target list (Week 0):** 20 mid-market Nairobi estates/areas — South B/C,
Umoja, Buruburu, Kasarani, Roysambu, Embakasi, Ruaka, Rongai, Kitengela,
Syokimau… — each with: parents' WhatsApp groups, a chief's camp/church/chama
network, cyber cafés and shops for posters, and 2–5 feeder schools.

**The three ground moves:**

1. **School-gate closing day (THIS WEEK).** Parents physically collect report
   cards on closing day — the one day they are all in one place, thinking about
   grades. Flyer + QR at the gate: *"Report card shock? See exactly which
   topics your child is missing — free, 15 minutes, on your phone."*
2. **Holiday Ambassadors — commission-only.** University students are ALSO on
   holiday and want money. Recruit 20 (KCSE alumni of target schools
   preferred). **No per-signup payout** (that needs capital). Instead:
   **20% of the first live lesson booked by any student they brought** —
   ≈ KSh 200 per converting student, paid AFTER the lesson money is in.
   Zero cash risk: every shilling paid out is a share of a shilling already
   received. Sweeten with free glory: top ambassador each week is named on
   the page, and the monthly champion gets KSh 2,000 — funded from lesson
   revenue, paid only if revenue exists.
3. **WhatsApp group seeding.** Ambassadors and early parents post the result
   card + link in estate/parent/chama/church groups. Never spam from unknown
   numbers — the *ambassador's mum* posting in her own chama group is the
   channel.

## 4. The drumbeat (weekly cycles)

Momentum needs a rhythm the groups can feel:

| Week | Cycle theme | The Friday event |
|---|---|---|
| **W0 (now)** | Closing-day gate blitz + ambassador recruiting | Ambassadors signed, groups mapped |
| **W1** | "Find your level" — diagnostic push | First draw winners announced (screenshots fly) |
| **W2** | "Streak Wars" — longest daily streak per estate | Streak champions + draw |
| **W3** | "Mastery Race" — most skills mastered | Champions + draw |
| **W4** | "Back-to-School Ready" — progress report cards | **Grand prize** — funded from August lesson revenue (target KSh 5–10k; if revenue is thin, the grand prize is a term of free live lessons donated by a partner tutor for exposure) + report card share wave |

Every Friday: winners posted on the Facebook page + in groups, with first
names + estate ("Brian from Umoja mastered 31 skills this week 🔥"). Social
proof compounding weekly.

## 5. The September conversion (the reason for all of it)

The blitz is not the business — **the school deal is the business**
(HOREB: KSh 500/student/term). The holiday cohort is the Trojan horse:

- Every active student generates a **progress report** naming their school.
- Cluster the data by school: *"41 of your students used Tutagora this
  holiday; here is the class-level gap analysis for your Form 2s."*
- Walk into the head teacher's office in the first week of Term 3 with THEIR
  OWN students' data. That is a warm pitch no cold email can match.
- Parents who watched their child improve all August are the loudest voice at
  the school gate — and the first buyers of live lessons.

## 7. Budget — zero-capital mode

The engine is designed so that **money only goes out after money comes in**:

| Item | KSh | Funded by |
|---|---|---|
| Ambassador commissions (20% of first lessons) | variable | **Lesson revenue** — paid after receipt, never before |
| Weekly airtime prizes (3 × 100 × 4 weeks) | 1,200 | Petty cash — or skip W1 and fund from first revenue |
| Print: 1,000 B/W flyers for closing-day gates | ~3,000 | Petty cash (WhatsApp poster is free and does most of the work) |
| Facebook boosts | 0 | Organic only — Friday winner posts + parent shares carry it |
| **Total cash at risk** | **≈ 4,200** (~$32) | Everything else is revenue-share |

Scale-up (optional, only from revenue): once live-lesson bookings flow, recycle
20–30% of gross into bigger prizes, colour posters, and boosted winner posts.
The campaign funds its own escalation — that's the discipline: **growth spend
is a percentage of revenue, never a bet against it.**

## 8. Targets & kill-switches

| Metric | W1 | W2 | W4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Activated students (diagnostic done) | 300 | 800 | 2,000 |
| Referral share of signups | 20% | 35% | 50% |
| D7 retention (any practice) | 35% | 40% | 45% |
| Estates with 30+ students | 3 | 8 | 15 |
| Schools with 20+ students (Sept pipeline) | 5 | 12 | 25 |

**Kill-switches:** if W2 activation < 400 → concentrate all ambassadors in the
3 best estates (density over spread). If referral share < 20% → the share
moment is broken; fix the product loop before recruiting another ambassador.
(With zero-capital mode there is no print/boost budget to cut — the only
resources are focus and the ambassadors' time. Spend them where it's working.)

## 9. What ships in the product (this commit)

1. **Tracked referrals** — real codes, captured at signup, counted on
   activation (diagnostic completion), visible to the user ("You've brought 4
   friends"). The old display-only link is replaced.
2. **WhatsApp share loop** — one-tap share with prefilled text + code at the
   two proudest moments: skill mastered and diagnostic completed.
3. **Holiday Challenge banner** — on the tutor home, with the weekly theme.
4. Ambassador tracking = the same referral codes (an ambassador is just a
   user with a code and hustle).

## 10. Security note (fix before launch)

`facebook-config.json` contains a **live page access token committed to the
repo**. Rotate that token in Meta Business settings and move it to an
environment variable before the campaign drives attention (and attackers) to
the page. Never commit the replacement.
