# Integrated Science Grade 8: outcomes a question cannot fully test (13 Nov 2026)

Built from `curriculum/integrated_science/g8.json` and the registry entry. Outcome text is KICD's, word for word. **Q** means a question can test it, **W** writing (composition marking), **O** speaking or an oral check (a teacher listening), **P** a practical task, product or portfolio, **A** an attitude (teacher observation).

- Outcomes: 37. By what tests them best: Q 25, W 0, O 0, P 4, A 8.
- Outcomes with at least one question family: 28. Q outcomes with a family: 25 of 25. Q outcomes with no family: 0.
- A non-Q outcome that has a family: the family tests the recognition half and the rest still needs the teacher or the task named below.
- Kind is assigned from the verb of the outcome (describe, explain, outline, identify, classify, assign and determine are Q; demonstrate and develop are P; appreciate is A). It is a first sort for the reviewer, not the KICD's own tag. P outcomes whose knowledge half has a family are marked "yes (knowledge half)".
- Medical and safety statements (the menstrual cycle, STIs and their prevention, fire classes and extinguishers) are written plainly for Grade 8 and are for a teacher or a health worker to confirm. The questions name a trusted adult or a health worker as the place to turn and never ask a child to light or fight a fire.
- Word equations, symbols and the magnification sum are tested by tapped options (Mathematics-style numbers are computed in the question).
