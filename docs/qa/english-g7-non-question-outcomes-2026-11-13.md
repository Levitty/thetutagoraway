# English Grade 7: outcomes questions cannot test (13 Nov 2026)

Written offline from `curriculum/english/g7.json` (KICD English, revised 2024; 254 outcomes). 130 outcomes sit on a skill that has question families (213 families on 71 skills, all draft, off for children). The other 124 need a person or a task, and each is listed below with its route. Composition marking uses the KICD rubrics kept in the file (ENGL.G7.RUBRIC.1.1 to 1.26) and PLAN_OBSERVATION_RUBRICS.md.

Counts by route: Q 130, W 14, O 23, P 16, A 71. The route of each outcome follows its verb (the first word of the official text); the list is for a teacher to correct.
