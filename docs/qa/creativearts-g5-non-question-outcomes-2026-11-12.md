# Creative Arts Grade 5: outcomes a question cannot fully test (12 Nov 2026)

Built from `curriculum/creative_arts/g5.json` and the registry entry. Outcome text is KICD's. **P** is a practical or product (make, draw, paint, carve, play, swim, perform, compose), **O** a singing or listening check, **A** an attitude or value (teacher observation or portfolio). All are scored by the rubrics in PLAN_OBSERVATION_RUBRICS.md (the KICD indicator for the outcome, four levels EE, ME, AE, BE, kept intact in the curriculum JSON); several are also KNEC school-based assessment projects and practicals for Grade 5 Creative Arts, which need the task, the score sheet and photo evidence of the work.

- Outcomes: 67. By how they are assessed: P 52, O 2, A 13. No outcome is purely a question outcome: this is a performance subject.
- Outcomes with at least one question family on the knowledge behind them: 40 of 67 (40 skills, 120 families, 127 misconceptions). Outcomes with no family: 27.
- A P outcome that has a family: the family tests the knowledge half (for example the order of the tie and dye steps, or which part of the foot traps the ball). The practical half still needs the task below. A child who passes the questions has not shown the skill.
- The pilot for observation is Science and Technology; Creative Arts needs the same fold-out (lesson observation) and task paper (rubric rows 1 to 4 per criterion, photo of the work) before any practical outcome here can be assessed in the system.
