# English G4, G6-G9 questions wave: items a Kenyan English teacher must check first (19 Nov 2026)

New draft families (all reviewed:false) are in the review packs `docs/qa/english-g<N>-review-2026-11-19.md`
(one line per family with a drawn sample). These items depend on local usage or on speech, so a teacher checks them before any sign-off:

- **G9 `english9.short.direct` and `english9.short.spot`**: the riddle opening "Kitendawili!" with the reply "Tega!" (taken from the map's misconception example). Confirm the form taught in English lessons.
- **G9 `english9.tags.*`**: the falling tune when sure and the rising tune when asking. The tune is heard by a teacher; the question only tests the rule.
- **G9 `english9.euph.*`**: euphemisms (passed away, senior citizen, washroom, between jobs, expecting) and "kicked the bucket" used as a mocking wrong answer.
- **G8 `english8.idiom.*`, G9 `english9.idiom.*`**: idioms in context (an arm and a leg, a blessing in disguise, all at sea, plain sailing, rock the boat, spill the beans, bury the hatchet).
- **G8 `english8.myth.*`**: the myths are original and not attributed to any community; check they read as myths a Grade 8 class would recognise.
- **G4 `english4.family.*`**: "younger/older brother" against "small/big brother", and "elder sister" accepted beside "older sister".
- **G4 `english4.sms.*`**: the short forms shown as wrong answers (gud mrng, 2mrw); confirm the line between common and unclear short forms.
- **G6 `english6.describe.*`**: the adjective order items (opinion, size, age, colour, material).
- **G7 `english7.author.who`, `english7.setting.*`**: every cover, title page and opening is invented; no real class reader is named.

Skills still without families in these grades are voice, composition or teacher skills (assessed_by in the map); their routes are in the existing non-question outcome lists.
