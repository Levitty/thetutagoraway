### integratedscience7.in.comp.direct

Skill `SCIENT.INTRODUCTION_TO_INTEGRAT.SCI_COMPONENTS`, outcome `a`, form direct, rung 1.

**Q:** Which statement about the branches of science is true?
Options: Each branch is completely separate | **Many topics use more than one branch** | The branches never share a topic | Only chemistry is used in the kitchen
- If a child answers "Each branch is completely separate": The branches are joined. Many topics use all three. Integrated Science joins the three branches. Many topics, such as digestion or cooking, use all three.
- If a child answers "The branches never share a topic": The branches are joined. Many topics use all three. Integrated Science joins the three branches. Many topics, such as digestion or cooking, use all three.
