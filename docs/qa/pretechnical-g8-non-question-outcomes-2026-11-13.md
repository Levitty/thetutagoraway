# Pre-Technical Studies Grade 8: outcomes a question cannot fully test (13 Nov 2026)

Built from `curriculum/pre_technical/g8.json` and the registry entry. Outcome text is KICD's, word for word. **Q** means a question can test it, **W** writing (composition marking), **O** speaking or an oral check (a teacher listening), **P** a practical task, product or portfolio, **A** an attitude (teacher observation).

- Outcomes: 60. By what tests them best: Q 36, W 0, O 0, P 11, A 13.
- Outcomes with at least one question family: 36. Q outcomes with a family: 36 of 36. Q outcomes with no family: 0.
- A non-Q outcome that has a family: the family tests the recognition half and the rest still needs the teacher or the task named below.
- Kind is assigned from the verb of the outcome (identify, describe, explain, outline, classify and similar are Q; extinguish, secure, construct, draw, create, use and prepare are P; appreciate, acknowledge, embrace, value and recognise are A). Recognising suitable markets is treated as P because it is a local visit or discussion. It is a first sort for the reviewer, not the KICD's own tag. P outcomes whose knowledge half has a family are marked "yes (knowledge half)".
- Fire-safety, data-safety, drawing and money statements are written plainly for Grade 8 and are for a teacher to confirm. The questions never ask a child to light or fight a fire.
- Sums on scale and dimensions are computed in the question from small whole numbers and carry their units.
