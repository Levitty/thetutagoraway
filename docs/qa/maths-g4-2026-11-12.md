## Hands-on outcomes: what a typed or tapped answer cannot test

- **Measure length in centimetres** (b): Measuring real objects with a ruler or tape belongs to class; the typed skill reads a ruler picture. The typed families test the thinking only.
- **One metre is 100 centimetres** (c): The design asks the learner to find the relationship practically by measuring a metre stick; the skill is the fact itself. The typed families test the thinking only.
- **Compare the area of surfaces** (a): Placing one surface on another is done with real surfaces in class. The typed families test the thinking only.
- **Pile objects into stacks of cubes and cuboids** (a): Piling real blocks or objects is done in class. The typed families test the thinking only.
- **Estimate time using a.m. and p.m.** (b): Using a shadow to estimate the time is done outdoors in class. The typed families test the thinking only.
- **Clockwise and anticlockwise turns** (a, b): Outcome b (demonstrate a turn) is done with the body in class. The typed families test the thinking only.
- **Quarter, half and full turns** (c, d): Outcome d (demonstrate the turns) is done with the body in class. The typed families test the thinking only.
- **Draw rectangles, squares, triangles, circles and ovals** (b): Drawing is done on paper in class; a typed answer cannot test it. No typed family: it stays with paper and the teacher.
- **Patterns with squares, rectangles and triangles** (d): Patterns are made with cut-outs in class; a typed answer cannot test it. No typed family: it stays with paper and the teacher.
- **Collect data of at most 10 items in school** (b): Collecting data is done at school; a typed answer cannot test it. No typed family: it stays with paper and the teacher.
