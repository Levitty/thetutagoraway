### agriculture9.hm.direct

**Q:** What is standing forage?
Options: **Grass left uncut to graze later** | Maize stover that is burnt | Grass cut and stood in heaps | Grass cut in the drought
- If a child answers "Maize stover that is burnt": Forage is conserved in the rainy season as hay, standing forage or stored crop residues, so animals stay strong at home through a drought. Keep maize stover dry as feed for the dry season.
- If a child answers "Grass cut and stood in heaps": Forage is conserved in the rainy season as hay, standing forage or stored crop residues, so animals stay strong at home through a drought. Standing forage is grass left growing in a closed paddock for the dry season.

### agriculture9.hk.direct

Skill `CONSER.CONSERVING_ANIMAL_FEED_H.HAYMAKING`, outcome `b`, form direct, rung 1.

**Q:** Why is grass not baled while still wet?
Options: It keeps better wet | It is too green to bale | Wet bales are easier to store outside | **It goes mouldy and heats up**
- If a child answers "It keeps better wet": Cut grass at flowering, dry it until dry but still green, bale or stack it on a raised base with a sloping top, and store it covered and off the ground. Dry hay well before baling. Damp hay goes mouldy.
- If a child answers "It is too green to bale": Cut grass at flowering, dry it until dry but still green, bale or stack it on a raised base with a sloping top, and store it covered and off the ground. Cut grass at flowering. Old brown grass has little feed value.
