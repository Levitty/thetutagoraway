## Not changed, noted for a teacher

Stems such as "Which of these is a proper noun." end with a full stop, not a question mark, throughout the pools (a fault of the item kit, not of single items). "Look out, the glass is falling" is keyed with an exclamation mark only. The sound items depend on accent. The questions about a speech, an introduction and a friendly letter follow one textbook convention.

## Safeguarding-sensitive items for a teacher before any child sees them

The Lost Fare (a friend takes savings to reach a sick mother), Fire at the Market (a fourteen-year-old fights a fire and sells for his aunt: safety advice for children is not given), a funeral as an occasion for a speech, and the introduction of a "guest of honour" are fine in tone but a teacher should read them. Nothing else is sensitive.
