# CRE Grade 5: outcomes questions cannot test, and what the teacher reviewer must confirm (12 Nov 2026)

Written with the build of CRE Grade 5 (`curriculum/cre/g5.json`, `cre_g5_*.js`). 115 official outcomes in 30 sub-strands. **83 are covered by question skills (35 skills, 70 families, 111 library entries).** The other **32 are not testable by a tapped answer**: 31 values and attitudes (A) and 1 practical (P). Nothing here is switched on for children: every family is `reviewed: false` and the signed list is empty.

## 1. Values and attitudes (A): teacher judgement, never scored

Report as descriptors (Exceeds, Meets, Approaches, Below, from the KICD rubric on each sub-strand) with a teacher comment, never as a test mark. What each needs:

- **Teacher observation over the term** (PLAN_OBSERVATION_RUBRICS.md): obeying, sharing, respecting the elderly, helping the needy, choosing friends, using social media responsibly, avoiding alcohol and drugs. One observed example is better than any answer a child could tap.
- **A reflection journal or portfolio line** where the design suggests one (helping those in need, how to choose good friends, the pure heart): the child writes or draws; the teacher reads it.
- **A class or school activity** where the design gives one (a talent show, a school notice-board message against drugs, a poem or song): done and seen, not tested.

## 3. Covered by questions, but the outcome asks the child to tell or retell

The questions test the facts of the story and its lessons. The outcome itself says narrate or retell, which a tapped answer cannot show: a teacher listens (the oral check the design suggests: in turns, in pairs, role-play) and ticks the facts the child gets right. Questions give the evidence; the telling is a speaking check.

## 4. What the CRE teacher reviewer must confirm

All wording is HOREB's, written offline from the passages the KICD design names. Please check, and say which Bible version the school reads (the questions use plain wording, not one translation).

1. **Lord's Supper (Luke 22:14-20).** Questions ask only what the passage says (the apostles, bread, thanks, "in remembrance of me", the new covenant) and that churches use different names (Lord's Supper, Lord's Table, Holy Communion, Eucharist). Nothing teaches how it works. Confirm this suits the school's tradition.
2. **Baptism of Jesus (Luke 3:21-22).** The library says Jesus was baptised to obey God and stand with the people, not to confess sin, and that baptism is a sign of repentance, new life and belonging to God. Churches word this differently; nothing says how or at what age. Confirm or reword (entry `M_BJ_SINNED`).
3. **Ten Commandments.** Never numbered, because churches number them differently. The questions cite Exodus 20:3-5 (no other gods, no idols) and Exodus 20:13 only.
4. **Gifts and fruit of the Spirit.** Gifts are the nine of 1 Corinthians 12:8-10; the fruit are the nine of Galatians 5:22-23 with "patience" (some versions "forbearance"). "Faith" and "wisdom" are avoided as options because they sit near both lists. Confirm wording against the school's Bible.
5. **Mount Carmel and false gods.** No religion is named. The harmful-group question tests a safety rule (a group that asks a child to keep secrets from parents), not another faith. Islamic and Hindu RE are not loaded anywhere.
6. **Human sexuality, alcohol and drugs, social media.** Written plainly for Grade 5: normal changes, who to ask, warning signs of an unhealthy friendship (secrecy, pressure, gifts for favours), and saying no. The Childline Kenya number 116 is named in the child-labour skill: confirm it is current. A child-development reviewer should look at these three sub-strands.
7. **Bible facts that need a check against the school's Bible:** Matthew 25:14-30 (five, two, one talents), Matthew 15:32-38 (seven loaves, seven baskets, not the five thousand), Genesis 9:18-23 (Shem, Ham, Japheth; Shem and Japheth covered Noah), 1 Kings 18 (450 prophets of Baal; twelve stones), Acts 12 (Rhoda), Daniel 5 (Belshazzar, not Nebuchadnezzar).
8. **Skill code prefix.** The curriculum codes are `CRE.G5...`, but the registry reads a four-capital prefix, so HOREB's skill codes start `CRES.G5...` (the official outcome codes are unchanged).
