## One drawn example per family

Read the question, the right answer and what a child is told after a wrong one. If a family is right and clear, its id goes in the signed list.

### cre8.so.direct

Skill `CREATI.ORIGIN_AND_CONSEQUENCES_.SIN_ORIGIN`, outcome `a`, form direct, rung 1.

**Q:** What did Adam and Eve eat that God had forbidden?
Options: Honey from the garden | Bread from the field | **Fruit of the tree of knowledge** | Fish from the river
- If a child answers "Honey from the garden": They were sent out of the garden of Eden. They ate the forbidden fruit.
- If a child answers "Bread from the field": They were sent out of the garden of Eden. The serpent tempted Eve.

### cre8.so.story

Skill `CREATI.ORIGIN_AND_CONSEQUENCES_.SIN_ORIGIN`, outcome `a`, form word, rung 2.

**Q:** A learner says sin began when God made Adam. What is true?
Options: She is right, God made sin | God made sin on the first day | **Sin began when they disobeyed God** | Sin always existed in Eden
- If a child answers "She is right, God made sin": They were sent out of the garden of Eden. They ate the forbidden fruit.
