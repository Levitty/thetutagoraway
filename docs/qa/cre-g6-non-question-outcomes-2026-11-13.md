# Christian Religious Education Grade 6: outcomes a question cannot fully test (13 Nov 2026)

Built from `curriculum/cre/g6.json` and the registry entry. Outcome text is KICD's, word for word. **Q** means a question can test it, **W** writing (composition marking), **O** speaking or an oral check (a teacher listening), **P** a practical task, product or portfolio, **A** an attitude (teacher observation).

- Outcomes: 84. By what tests them best: Q 45, W 0, O 15, P 0, A 24.
- Outcomes with at least one question family: 55. Q outcomes with a family: 38 of 45. Q outcomes with no family: 7.
- A non-Q outcome that has a family: the family tests the recognition half and the rest still needs the teacher or the task named below.
- Kind is assigned from the verb of the outcome (retell, narrate, discuss and recite are O, desire, appreciate, trust, emulate, depend on and the like are A). It is a first sort for the reviewer, not the KICD's own tag.
- Bible facts asked are those stated in the passages the design names. Nothing denominational: the Ten Commandments are not numbered, nothing is asked on how Communion or baptism is done, the creed is asked only for what its words say. Islamic and Hindu RE are not loaded.
- The sensitive units (human sexuality, sanctity of life, alcohol and drugs, early marriage) are written for Grade 6 with a trusted adult always named as the place to turn; a CRE teacher and the child developmental psychology reviewer should read those families first.
