# Outcome coverage, English Grade 5 (2026-11-12)

Generated by `npm run coverage:english` (`scripts/coverage-english.mjs`). Free and local: no model calls. Counts and outcome codes only.

Official outcomes come from `curriculum/english/g5.json` (200 outcomes in 52 sub-strands, all KICD text). Every outcome has one **route**: a question **family** (114 draft families in the bank, none switched on for children), **composition marking** (HOREB's English composition marker, four bands out of 20), **voice or a teacher** (speaking, listening and sounds, which a typed answer cannot test), **a teacher only** (reading habits, handwriting, forms, opinions) or **attitude** (an outcome about caring, advocating or recommending).
