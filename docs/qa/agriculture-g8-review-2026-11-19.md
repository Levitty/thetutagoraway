### agriculture8.sc.direct

Skill `CONSER.SOIL_CONSERVATION_MEASUR.SOIL_CONSERVE`, outcome `a`, form direct, rung 1.

**Q:** What causes soil erosion on bare land?
Options: **Running water and wind** | Planting trees | Mulching | Terracing
- If a child answers "Planting trees": Conservation slows water and wind. Terraces slow water and hold soil.
- If a child answers "Mulching": Conservation slows water and wind. Mulch covers and protects soil.
