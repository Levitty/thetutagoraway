# Kiswahili Grade 4: what a native speaker must check, and what questions cannot test (13 Nov 2026)

Written offline by Claude; no model was called for the content. **40 skills, 136 library entries, 195 question families**, all drafts and **switched off for children**. The signed list `kiswahili_g4_signed.js` is empty and `native: true` is set on every family: every item is in Kiswahili sanifu at a nine-year-old's level, with original passages and Kenyan names, and every item needs a native speaker who teaches Kiswahili. Nothing reaches a child until that person adds family ids to the signed list. The review pack `kiswahili-g4-review-2026-11-13.md` shows one drawn example of every family. The routes note `kiswahili-g4-routes-2026-11-13.md` lists every outcome no family carries and how it is assessed.

Misconception codes start `M4_`, so the joined Kiswahili map (Grades 4 and 5) never clashes with Grade 5.

**The misconception library** (`curriculum/kiswahili/g4.json`, 136 entries, all `draft`): each has the wrong idea, the revealing question, the repair, the line the child is told in Kiswahili, and a source. The source is "common learner error; to confirm by a native Kiswahili teacher" (or English or mother-tongue influence). Please mark each entry as real, rare or wrong, and add the errors you see every week that are missing.

**Signing:** add the ids of the families you accept to `src/ai-tutor/content/families/kiswahili_g4_signed.js` (`export const SIGNED = [...]`). Nobody else signs. A signed family reaches children only when the school door for Kiswahili is also on.

## 2. Known limits of this build (nothing to fix in the content)

- **A passage needs a screen.** Passage families carry `passage: { title, text }` on the problem, as English and Kiswahili Grade 5 do. The screen that shows it is still to be built before any such family is signed on.
- **The spot-the-mistake frame is in English** ("Step 1" to "Step 4"), because it comes from `families/core.js`, a shared file. The explanation after it is in Kiswahili.
- **Capital letters are not marked in typed answers.** The word marker folds case. A tapped option is matched exactly, so the uakifishi families (whole sentences) mark capitals and punctuation.
- **Accepted alternatives are authored per item** (for example raha or shangwe for furaha). Please add the alternatives a teacher would accept.
- **A few families have only two named wrong answers** (mnyambuliko, nyakati, LI-LI, aina za insha, kamusi). They are marked thinOk. More distractors would help once the reviewer has chosen the words.
- **"Kutunga" and "kutumia" outcomes are tested by a proxy.** Where an outcome says compose or use in a sentence, the question asks the child to choose or type the word that completes a sentence. It does not test writing a whole sentence.
- **Seventeen question outcomes have a skill but no family tagged to their letter** (mostly "explain the meaning" and the kinds of insha that the design repeats in later themes). The routes note lists them.

## 3. Outcomes a question cannot test

Of 210 official outcomes, 94 are carried by at least one family. The other 116 are in the routes note, each with its route:

| Class | Outcomes | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| W, writing (insha, barua, tongue-twisters) | 11 | Composition marking: HOREB insha marker out of 20 (waits for native-speaker review) or a teacher with the KICD rubric |
| O, speaking, listening, reading aloud | 24 | A teacher listening (speaking check or reading aloud), with the KICD rubric; a voice tool later |
| P, practical or library | 13 | Computer and digital-device tasks with a teacher watching; a reading log or portfolio for choosing texts |
| A, attitudes | 51 | One termly teacher line per learner, exceptions only; never a question |
| Q, no family tagged | 17 | A skill carries them; a teacher may add a direct family |

The KICD rubrics in the curriculum file are kept as they were.
