# Creative Arts and Sports Grade 8: outcomes a question cannot fully test (13 Nov 2026)

Built from `curriculum/creative_arts/g8.json` and the registry entry. Outcome text is KICD's, word for word. **Q** means a question can test it, **W** writing (composition marking), **O** speaking or an oral check (a teacher listening), **P** a practical task, product or portfolio, **A** an attitude (teacher observation).

- Outcomes: 71. By what tests them best: Q 18, W 0, O 0, P 37, A 16.
- Outcomes with at least one question family: 25. Q outcomes with a family: 17 of 18. Q outcomes with no family: 1.
- A non-Q outcome that has a family: the family tests the recognition half and the rest still needs the teacher or the task named below.
- Kind is assigned from the first verb of the outcome (describe, outline, explain, identify, classify and similar are Q; make, draw, paint, compose, carve, perform, execute, create, sing, play, write and name pitches are P; appreciate, enjoy, realise and recognise are A). It is a first sort for the reviewer, not the KICD's own tag.
- Javelin, swimming, handball and football are shown by a teacher or coach in a safe place; questions ask for names, order and rules only. Music answers (note values, treble staff) are standard staff notation facts, to be confirmed by a music teacher.
