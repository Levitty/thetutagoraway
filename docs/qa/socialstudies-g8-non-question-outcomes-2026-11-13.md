# Social Studies Grade 8: outcomes a question cannot fully test (13 Nov 2026)

Built from `curriculum/social_studies/g8.json` and the registry entry. Outcome text is KICD's, word for word. **Q** means a question can test it, **W** writing (composition marking), **O** speaking or an oral check (a teacher listening), **P** a practical task, product or portfolio, **A** an attitude (teacher observation).

- Outcomes: 79. By what tests them best: Q 40, W 0, O 0, P 20, A 19.
- Outcomes with at least one question family: 47. Q outcomes with a family: 37 of 40. Q outcomes with no family: 3.
- A non-Q outcome that has a family: the family tests the recognition half and the rest still needs the teacher or the task named below.
- Kind is assigned from the verb of the outcome (describe, explain, identify, outline, explore, examine and illustrate are Q; implement, design, plan, report, apply, assess and manage are P; appreciate, desire, recognise, show empathy and exhibit are A). It is a first sort for the reviewer, not the KICD's own tag.
- The people and history units are told as commonly taught and are marked "to confirm by a teacher": human origin and early humans, the Swahili city-states, the Trans-Saharan trade, historical sites. Figures (the 2019 census, the 6.5 degrees per 1000 metres) are rounded.
- The map area questions give a count of 1 km squares; real topographical sheets and the marginal information are practical work with the sheet in hand.
