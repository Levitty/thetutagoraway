# CRE Grade 4: outcomes questions cannot test, and what the teacher reviewer must confirm (13 Nov 2026)

Written with the build of CRE Grade 4 (`curriculum/cre/g4.json`, `cre_g4_*.js`). The design file holds **158 outcome entries in 32 sub-strands**. 29 of them are the same outcome read twice by the text layer (the letters repeat, sometimes with part of the line cut); they are kept word for word and are counted below. **107 are covered by question skills (39 skills, 78 families, 118 library entries).** The other **51 are not testable by a tapped answer**. Nothing here is switched on for children: every family is `reviewed: false` and the signed list is empty.

Report A, O and P outcomes as the KICD descriptors (Exceeds, Meets, Approaches, Below, from the rubric on each strand) with a teacher comment, never as a test mark. The questions give evidence of knowing the Bible text; the child's faith, prayer and worship are never scored.

## What the teacher reviewer must confirm

- **Bible wording.** Verses are quoted in the plain sense of a common modern translation. Where translations differ the questions avoid the difference: Proverbs 1:7 (knowledge or wisdom: the design says wisdom, the question accepts both), Galatians 5:22-23 (patience or longsuffering, gentleness or meekness, self-control or temperance), Matthew 6:9-13 (the lines of the Lord's Prayer: the questions ask what each line asks for, never the exact words), Luke 1:37 (nothing is impossible, or no word from God will fail). Confirm that the school's translation does not contradict a keyed answer.
- **Every Bible fact asked is stated in the passage the design names.** Checked against: Luke 19:2-10, Numbers 22:1-6 and 20-34, Judges 14:5-9, Genesis 41:14-24 and 28-30, 12:1-5, 17:1-5, Luke 1:5-13, 57, 76-79 and 1:26-37, Mark 10:46-52, Luke 17:11-19, Luke 7:11-16, John 11:25, Matthew 5:39, 5:42, Luke 15:8-10, 13:18-19, John 3:1-5 and 3:16, Genesis 39:7-12, Psalm 125:1, Proverbs 12:19, Ephesians 4:25, Exodus 20:12, Matthew 19:13-15, 1 Corinthians 6:19-20, Matthew 21:12-14, 10:1-4, Acts 2:38-41, 7:54-60, 11:26, Galatians 5:22-25, Ephesians 4:26, Philippians 4:4, Psalm 139:13-14, Genesis 1:27, Ephesians 6:1-3, 1 John 4:7-8, 1 Samuel 2:2. A reviewer should still check each keyed answer against the school's Bible.
- **No denominational claims.** Nothing asks how baptism is done, what the Lord's Supper means, how many books the Bible has (traditions differ), or what any tradition teaches beyond the text. Baptism is mentioned only as Acts 2:38 words it; its importance (Nicodemus c) is left to a teacher of the school's tradition. Islamic and Hindu RE were not loaded.
- **Child protection items need a safeguarding reviewer.** The families `cre4.protection.*` and `cre4.safe.*` (and the matching library entries) teach: private parts are private, say NO, go away, tell a trusted adult, a secret about touching is never kept, it is never the child's fault, and the Childline Kenya helpline is 116. Confirm the helpline number and the wording with a child-protection specialist before anything is signed. The design also cites 2 Samuel 13:4-14 for this sub-strand; no question uses that passage because its content is unsuitable for a tapped question, and the teacher should decide how to treat it.
- **Misconception library.** Each entry's source is "common learner error; to confirm by a CRE teacher" or a story-mixing note. None is from learner data yet.
- **Repeated outcomes.** The design's text layer repeated some letters (the second read flagged them). The copies are kept so no official text is lost; skills cover every copy that carries the same letter. In Self-awareness and My Family the repeats are real, separate groups (feelings and choices; protection), and the skills cover them by their exact code.
- **Reading level.** Questions and options are written for a nine-year-old and checked by the pre-review reading test; a teacher should read a few drawn items aloud with Grade 4 learners.
