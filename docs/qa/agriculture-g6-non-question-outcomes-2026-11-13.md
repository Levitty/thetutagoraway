# Agriculture and Nutrition Grade 6: outcomes a question cannot fully test (13 Nov 2026)

Built from `curriculum/agriculture/g6.json` and the registry entry. Outcome text is KICD's, word for word. **Q** means a question can test it, **W** writing (composition marking), **O** speaking or an oral check (a teacher listening), **P** a practical task, product or portfolio, **A** an attitude (teacher observation).

- Outcomes: 30. By what tests them best: Q 10, W 0, O 0, P 10, A 10.
- Outcomes with at least one question family: 11. Q outcomes with a family: 10 of 10. Q outcomes with no family: 0.
- A non-Q outcome that has a family: the family tests the recognition half and the rest still needs the teacher or the task named below.
- Kind is assigned from the verb of the outcome (describe, identify and explain are Q; control, prepare, establish, rear, preserve, cook, practise, remove, use and construct are P; appreciate, embrace and adopt are A). It is a first sort for the reviewer, not the KICD's own tag. P outcomes whose knowledge half has a family are marked "yes (knowledge half)".
- Only the knowledge half of the practical outcomes is tested by questions. The work itself (terraces, seedbeds, deterrents, animals, drying, cooking, laundry, crochet, moist bed gardens) stays with the teacher and the school farm.
- Stain treatments, the Kenyan foods named with each cooking method and the deterrents named for each animal are for a teacher of Agriculture and Nutrition to confirm.
