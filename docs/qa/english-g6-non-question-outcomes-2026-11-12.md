# English Grade 6: outcomes questions cannot test (12 Nov 2026)

Written offline from `curriculum/english/g6.json` (KICD English, revised 2024; 194 outcomes). 122 outcomes sit on a skill that has question families (all draft, off for children). The other 72 need a person or a task. Each needs the thing named below. Composition marking uses the KICD rubrics kept in the file and PLAN_OBSERVATION_RUBRICS.md.

## W: composition marking (12 outcomes; the same rubric: topic, organisation, language, creativity)
- Planning: WRITIN.T2.a (topics and ideas), WRITIN.T5.a (plan collaboratively), WRITIN.T6.a, WRITIN.T8.a, WRITIN.T10.b. Needs a plan sheet read by the teacher.
- Creating: WRITIN.T2.c (open-ended), WRITIN.T4.b (formal invitation letter), WRITIN.T5.c and WRITIN.T8.c (narrative), WRITIN.T6.c (pictorial composition), WRITIN.T10.c (descriptive), WRITIN.T3.b (a story with acronyms, abbreviations and numerals). Needs the composition marker (HOREB marker exists, not in Tuta) or a teacher with the rubric.
- Judging a composition: WRITIN.T5.d, WRITIN.T6.d, WRITIN.T8.d, WRITIN.T10.d. Needs the learner's written comment on a sample, marked with the rubric.
- WRITIN.T10.a (adjectives for a person, object or place): the choice of adjective is tested by the sound-alike and mismatch items in the families only in part; the use is in the descriptive composition.

## O: speaking check (about 19 outcomes)
- Pronunciation and sounds: LISTEN.T3.a, LISTEN.T7.a, LISTEN.T8.b (the written sound-matching families test knowing the sound, not saying it; they are for a reviewer to confirm for the accent taught).
- Stress, intonation and rhythm: LISTEN.T4.c, LISTEN.T4.d, LISTEN.T6.c. Stress cannot be marked from a tapped answer (the answer check cannot tell REcord from reCORD), so a teacher listens.
- Oral presentation and fluency: LISTEN.T9.c, LISTEN.T12.b, READIN.T8.b and READIN.T8.c (reading speed, accuracy and expression). Needs a recorded or live reading with the fluency rubric.
- Conversation: LISTEN.T7.b (seek clarification, express moods), LISTEN.T10.c (moods), LISTEN.T12.c (non-verbal cues), LISTEN.T12.d (judge non-verbal cues on a video). The polite-talk families test the words; the voice and the face need a teacher.
- Listening tasks: LISTEN.T5.b (respond to oral questions), LISTEN.T6.b, LISTEN.T11.a, LISTEN.T12.a, LISTEN.T13.b (listen and rewrite: a dictation marked by the teacher). Needs a read-aloud text and an answer sheet.

## P: reading practice and teacher observation (about 18 outcomes)
- Choosing and reading books: READIN.T1.b, READIN.T5.b, READIN.T7.b, READIN.T8.a, READIN.T13.b. The reading log or portfolio shows it; the choice itself is partly tested in the suitable-text families.
- Connections to life: READIN.T6.a, READIN.T9.a, READIN.T10.a. Teacher judgement of a spoken or written answer.
- Visuals: READIN.T4.a (predict from visuals), READIN.T4.d (assess a visual). Needs the picture; HOREB has no picture-based English item yet.
- Word spotting in a digital text: READIN.T3.a, READIN.T11.a. Needs the digital text on screen and a teacher or a highlight task.
- LISTEN.T4.d and READIN.T12.d also appear above for stress and rhythm.

## What the families do cover (122 outcomes on 50 skills)
Grammar and language use (16 skills), writing mechanics, spelling, forms, formal letters, journals and paragraphs (12), proverbs, riddles, idioms, similes, phrasal verbs and theme words (8), sounds and polite talk (2), fact and opinion, reference books and choosing a text (3), and reading comprehension on original passages and poems (9). A question tests the knowledge that sits under each of the speaking and writing outcomes; it never replaces the speaking check or the composition marking.
