# Pre-Technical Studies Grade 9: outcomes a question cannot fully test (13 Nov 2026)

Built from `curriculum/pre_technical/g9.json` and the registry entry. Outcome text is KICD's, word for word. **Q** means a question can test it, **W** writing (composition marking), **O** speaking or an oral check (a teacher listening), **P** a practical task, product or portfolio, **A** an attitude (teacher observation).

- Outcomes: 55. By what tests them best: Q 27, W 0, O 0, P 18, A 10.
- Outcomes with at least one question family: 31. Q outcomes with a family: 27 of 27. Q outcomes with no family: 0.
- A non-Q outcome that has a family: the family tests the recognition half and the rest still needs the teacher or the task named below.
- Kind is assigned from the first verb of the outcome (identify, describe, explain, classify, analyse and similar are Q; observe, handle, choose, sketch, draw, create, use, dispose, select, make, utilise and fill in are P; appreciate, acknowledge and embrace are A). It is a first sort for the reviewer, not the KICD's own tag.
- Safety, tax and finance statements (VAT, PAYE, customs duty, SACCOs, eCitizen) are for a Pre-Technical teacher to confirm. No task with height, substances or tools is asked of a child as an action.
