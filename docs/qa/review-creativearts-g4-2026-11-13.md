## Anthem facts flagged

The East African Community anthem items say it asks God to bless East Africa with peace and unity, and that it is sung in unison, standing. I believe the words ask God to protect the Community and to let its people live in peace, so "bless" is close but not exact, and some arrangements are sung in parts. The Kenya National Anthem items (three verses, begins in Kiswahili, a prayer for blessing, peace and unity, stand still) match what I know. ## Keep, with a teacher note

All other families were true and at level. One general point on the spot-the-mistake families: in some, an earlier step states the wrong action (for example "She picks up a stick at once") and a later step states the claim (for example "it is safe"), and the key is the later one. A child who picks the action step is not clearly wrong. I left these, and a teacher may want the action step keyed instead. Points for the CBC teacher reviewer are in `TEACHER_QUESTIONS_13NOV.md`.
