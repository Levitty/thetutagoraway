Built from `curriculum/creative_arts/g6.json` and the registry entry. Outcome text is KICD's, word for word. **Q** means a question can test it, **W** writing (composition marking), **O** speaking or an oral check (a teacher listening), **P** a practical task, product or portfolio, **A** an attitude (teacher observation).

- Outcomes: 59. By what tests them best: Q 17, W 0, O 0, P 29, A 13.
- Outcomes with at least one question family: 21. Q outcomes with a family: 17 of 17. Q outcomes with no family: 0.
- A non-Q outcome that has a family: the family tests the recognition half and the rest still needs the teacher or the task named below.
- Kind is assigned from the verb of the outcome (identify, describe, classify, interpret and discuss are Q; make, paint, mount, compose, improvise, print, weave, perform, play, model and draw are P; value and appreciate are A). It is a first sort for the reviewer, not the KICD's own tag. P outcomes whose knowledge half has a family are marked "yes (knowledge half)".
- Only the knowledge half of the subject is tested by questions: parts and kinds of instruments, notation and sol-fa, colour words, steps of a technique, rules and safety. The KICD school-based projects (swimming, athletics, volleyball, gymnastics, pottery, weaving, printing, the recorder) stay practical.
- Instrument and community facts (nyatiti, orutu, litungu, obokano) and the sol-fa hand signs are for a teacher who knows them to confirm.
