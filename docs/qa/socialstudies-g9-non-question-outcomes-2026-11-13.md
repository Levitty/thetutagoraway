Built from `curriculum/social_studies/g9.json` and the registry entry. Outcome text is KICD's, word for word. **Q** means a question can test it, **W** writing (composition marking), **O** speaking or an oral check (a teacher listening), **P** a practical task, product or portfolio, **A** an attitude (teacher observation).

- Outcomes: 86. By what tests them best: Q 41, W 0, O 0, P 26, A 19.
- Outcomes with at least one question family: 43. Q outcomes with a family: 37 of 41. Q outcomes with no family: 4.
- A non-Q outcome that has a family: the family tests the recognition half and the rest still needs the teacher or the task named below.
- Kind is assigned from the first verb of the outcome (explain, identify, describe, examine, outline and similar are Q; design, construct, apply, implement, write, participate and similar are P; appreciate, value, embrace and similar are A). It is a first sort for the reviewer, not the KICD's own tag.
- Facts (Aswan, Kariba, the 2010 Constitution, Chapter 4, the three pathways) are those taught in Kenyan schools, to be confirmed by a Social Studies teacher. Political content is limited to the structure of the Constitution and elections, with no party or person named.
