# Agriculture and Nutrition Grade 5: outcomes a question cannot fully test (12 Nov 2026)

Built from `curriculum/agriculture/g5.json` and the registry entry. Outcome text is KICD's. **Q** means a question can test it, **A** an attitude (teacher observation or portfolio), **P** a practical or product: the learner builds, prepares, cooks, cleans, stitches or establishes something, which only a teacher can see. The kind is read from the outcome's verb. Per PLAN_OBSERVATION_RUBRICS.md a P outcome is a **task** marked by the teacher against the KICD rubric indicator (EE, ME, AE, BE), with an optional **photo of the work, not the child** as evidence. Agriculture is one of the learning areas for which KNEC sets school-based assessment projects and practicals at Grade 4 and 5, so each P task below can double as an SBA task once the KNEC tool is seen (the criteria and marks come from the KNEC scoring guide, not from this table).

- Outcomes: 42. By what tests them best: Q 16, A 12, P 14 (33 percent practical at this grade; about 42 percent across Grades 4 to 9).
- Outcomes with at least one question family: 30. With no family: 12. Q outcomes with no family: 0.
- A P outcome that has a family: the family tests the knowledge half (for example, which things go in the pit, which stitch is strong) and the practical half still needs the task below.
- The KICD rubrics in the curriculum JSON are untouched: 4 strand tables, 11 indicators (AGRI.G5.RUBRIC.1 to 4).
