Built from `curriculum/agriculture/g8.json` and the registry entry. Outcome text is KICD's, word for word. **Q** means a question can test it, **W** writing (composition marking), **O** speaking or an oral check (a teacher listening), **P** a practical task, product or portfolio, **A** an attitude (teacher observation).

- Outcomes: 41. By what tests them best: Q 12, W 0, O 0, P 17, A 12.
- Outcomes with at least one question family: 17. Q outcomes with a family: 12 of 12. Q outcomes with no family: 0.
- A non-Q outcome that has a family: the family tests the recognition half and the rest still needs the teacher or the task named below.
- Kind is assigned from the first verb of the outcome (explain, describe, identify and similar are Q; control, construct, conserve, plant, prepare, cook, launder, knit and make are P; adopt, appreciate and embrace are A). It is a first sort for the reviewer, not the KICD's own tag.
- Heat, chemicals and caustic soap ingredients are never asked of a child as an action; questions ask for names and reasons only. Facts are to be confirmed by an Agriculture teacher.
