# Outcome coverage, Mathematics Grades 1 to 9 (2026-11-13)

Generated by `npm run coverage:outcomes` (`scripts/coverage-outcomes.mjs`). Free and local: no model calls. Counts and outcome codes only.

Official outcomes come from `curriculum/mathematics/g<N>.json`. A **family** is a hand-written question family (1664 in the bank, 52 switched on for children, the rest waiting for a teacher). A **generator skill** is a skill in the practice engine tagged to the outcome. **Misconception feedback** means a draw carries a message for a named wrong answer. **Worked example** means the skill draws one. Coverage is of what exists in the bank, signed off or not.

Grades 1 to 3 are tagged to the sub-strand, not to an outcome, so they show no outcome-level cover here; the last column counts the outcomes whose sub-strand has any family or skill.
