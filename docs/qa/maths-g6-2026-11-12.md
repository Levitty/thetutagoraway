## What changed in the outcome letters

HOREB's earlier Grade 6 outcome list (`curriculum/outcomes.js`, `G6.<SUBSTRAND>.<letter>`) was condensed from the design and drifted from the official letters in places: it joined outcomes (add and subtract fractions as one letter, add and subtract mixed numbers as one, profit and loss as one, bisect and perpendicular as one), shifted letters in Length, Mass and Decimals, and had no separate letters for several official outcomes. The official codes in `curriculum/mathematics/g6.json` are now the only ones the Grade 6 families use (`MATH.G6.<STRAND>.<SUB-STRAND>.<letter>`). The old list is untouched, so nothing that read it breaks.

## How to read the sections

Each sub-strand lists its official outcomes, then each skill with the mastery rule, the question forms the child meets, the prerequisites (G5 or G6 skills), the misconceptions with where they come from, and the families that carry them. A misconception marked "no wrong answer yet" is in the library for a teacher to confirm; no question tests it today. The source line is either "a familiar error type in maths education (no single source cited)" or "to be confirmed by a teacher": no misconception here cites a specific study, because none was checked. Please strike any a Kenyan teacher does not recognise, and add the ones missing.

## 1.1 Whole Numbers (Numbers)

Design notes: Official outcomes a to g read word for word. The ranges are uneven in the design: a and b go up to millions, c and d only up to 100,000, e up to 100,000 rounded to the nearest thousand. Outcome h is attitudinal and is not mapped to a skill.

### Place value of digits up to millions

- Code `NUMBER.WHOLE_NUMBERS.PLACE_VALUE_MILLIONS`; outcome a; HOREB skill G6_PLACE_VALUE.
- Mastery: Given a whole number up to 9,999,999, the child names the place of any digit (ones to millions), including digits beside zeros, and says which digit is in a named place. Numbers in real settings such as a county population or a school budget.
- Question forms: name the place of a digit; which digit is in the given place; read a place value chart; missing digit from clues.
- Prerequisites: G5.NUMBER.WHOLE_NUMBERS.PLACE_VALUE_HTH (essential).

- `NUMBER.WHOLE_NUMBERS.READ_WRITE_WORDS_100K.M_TEEN_TY_SWAP`: Confuses the teens and the -ty numbers when moving between words and figures.
- `NUMBER.WHOLE_NUMBERS.SQUARE_ROOTS_10000.M_ROOT_OF_HUNDREDS`: Takes the root of a square ending in zeros as ten times too big or too small.
- `NUMBER.DIVISION.DIV_4D_BY_UP_TO_3D.M_DIVIDE_SMALL_BY_BIG`: Divides the smaller digit by the larger divisor part the wrong way round (for example 4 by 12 as 12 by 4).
- `NUMBER.DIVISION.COMBINED_OPERATIONS.M_DIVIDE_AFTER_MULTIPLY`: Always multiplies before dividing, even when the division comes first.
- `NUMBER.DIVISION.COMBINED_OPERATIONS.M_SUBTRACT_BEFORE_MULTIPLY`: Subtracts before multiplying when the subtraction comes first in the line.
- `NUMBER.FRACTIONS.ADD_FRACTIONS_LCM.M_PRODUCT_NOT_LCM`: Uses the product of the denominators instead of the LCM, so the answer is correct but not in simplest form, or the child stops early.
- `NUMBER.FRACTIONS.SUBTRACT_FRACTIONS_LCM.M_SMALLER_FROM_LARGER_TOP`: Subtracts the numerators without first renaming the second fraction with the common denominator.
- `NUMBER.DECIMALS.DECIMAL_TO_PERCENT.M_SMALL_DECIMAL_WRONG`: Writes 0.05 as 50% (or 0.5 as 5%) by losing a zero.
- `NUMBER.DECIMALS.SUBTRACT_DECIMALS_4DP.M_UNEQUAL_LENGTH_LEFT`: Subtracts a shorter decimal without writing zeros, so lines up the wrong digits.
- `NUMBER.INEQUALITIES.SOLVE_INEQUALITY.M_ONE_SOLUTION_ONLY`: Gives a single value as the solution rather than the set of values.
- `MEASUR.LENGTH.MM_CM_RELATIONSHIP.M_MM_AS_DECIMAL_CM`: Writes 25 mm as 2.5 cm and then reads it as 2 cm 5 mm in the wrong way round, or as 25 cm.
- `MEASUR.LENGTH.ADD_CM_MM.M_ADD_AS_DECIMAL`: Adds the numbers as decimals (3.8 + 1.6 = 5.4) and reads the answer as 5 cm 4 mm without noticing the renaming.
- `MEASUR.LENGTH.SUBTRACT_CM_MM.M_RENAME_NOT_REDUCED`: Adds 10 mm but does not take 1 cm off the cm column.
- `MEASUR.LENGTH.DIVIDE_CM_MM.M_UNIT_NOT_CHANGED_BACK`: Divides correctly in mm but gives the answer in cm, or the reverse.
- `MEASUR.AREA.AREA_TRIANGLE.M_SLANT_SIDE_AS_HEIGHT`: Uses the slanting side instead of the perpendicular height.
- `MEASUR.CAPACITY.CM3_ML_LITRE_RELATION.M_CM3_NOT_CAPACITY`: Believes cm3 measures length or area, not capacity.
- `MEASUR.MASS.CONVERT_KG_TONNES.M_TONNES_AS_KG_TEN`: Multiplies tonnes by 10 to get kg.

- `MEASUR.MASS.DIVIDE_TONNES_KG.M_DIVIDE_THE_WRONG_WAY_ROUND`: Divides the number of parts by the mass.
- `MEASUR.TIME.AM_PM_IDENTIFY.M_NOON_IS_AM`: Believes the hours just before and after noon are both a.m.

## What is not here

- Reciprocal, squares of fractions and several other outcomes were marked "read" (not fully verified) by the reading step; they are listed in the design notes above and a person should confirm the limits.
- Hands-on outcomes (draw parallel lines, bisect, construct, measure with a protractor, circumference with string, piling) cannot be marked by a typed or tapped answer. The skills test the idea behind each practical; the practical stays with the teacher.
- Grade 6 skills are not yet offered for homework: the homework door reads the Grade 5 map for Maths. That is the next step once a teacher has signed a first set.
- No question is signed. The older Grade 6 families keep their wrong-answer reasons; their wrong answers are tied to the library at family level (`reveals`), not one by one.
