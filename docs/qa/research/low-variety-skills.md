# Curriculum research: the four skills still under 20 different questions

Research, 30 Sep 2026. Question: what do the curricula actually ask for in
these skills, so new questions are curriculum-backed, not invented?

**Source caveat.** Every KICD and Cambridge site was blocked from the research
environment. Tags: [V] verified from an official document, [S] search snippet
only, [P] publisher material seen as a snippet, [K] researcher's recollection.
One finding was verified here from the official KICD Grade 8 design in
`docs/curriculum-sources/`. The rest still needs the official PDFs checked by
hand: KICD Grade 2, 3 and 6 designs and the Grade 11 design, the Cambridge
0096 framework and the 9709 syllabus.

## G6_INTEGERS_INTRO: more room than we use, but it is a Grade 8 topic in Kenya
- **[V] KICD Grade 8, 1.1 Integers (6 lessons):**
  - identify integers in different situations
  - represent integers on a number line in different situations
  - add and subtract integers on the number line in real-life situations
  - Learning experiences: climbing upstairs (+) and downstairs (−); standing
    at a zero point and counting steps forward or back; jumping games on a
    number line.
- **[V]** The KICD Grade 7 design has no integers sub-strand.
- **[S]** Real-life contexts in snippets: profit/loss, deposits/withdrawals,
  above/below sea level.
- **[S]** Cambridge Stage 6: temperature-change problems.
- **[K]** Cambridge 5Ni/6Ni: add and subtract integers.
- **Supported question types:**
  1. read the integer at a marked point
  2. integer from a real situation (a loss of sh 6 is −6; 5 m below sea level is −5)
  3. start at −3 and move 7 steps right
  4. temperature change
  5. compare two integers (implied rather than stated)
- **Grading issue:** our skill is tagged Grade 6. For CBC it belongs to Grade 8
  (Cambridge Stage 5-6 is fine).

## G3_POSITION: our turn questions look like Kenya Grade 4, not Grade 3
- **[P]** Kenya Grade 3: walk in a straight line and turn left or right on
  instructions. No fractions of a turn.
- **[S]** Kenya Grade 4: clockwise and anticlockwise; quarter, half and full turns.
- **[S]** Cambridge Stage 2 (2Gg.11): whole, half and quarter turns, clockwise
  and anticlockwise. A right angle is a quarter turn.
- **[S]** Cambridge Stage 3 (3Gp.01): cardinal points.
- **Supported question types:**
  1. clockwise or anticlockwise?
  2. anticlockwise quarter/half/full turns
  3. turn left or right (Kenya Grade 3)
  4. what you face after a turn
  5. a right angle is a quarter turn
  6. compass points after a turn (Cambridge Stage 3)
- **Grading issue:** three-quarter turns were found only in UK Year 2 sources.

## G2_FRACTIONS: probably halves and quarters only; add shapes, not new fractions
- **[S]** Kenya Grade 3: ½, ¼ and ⅛ as part of a whole and of a group,
  learnt by folding. So ⅛ starts in Grade 3.
- **[K]** Kenya Grade 2: ½ and ¼ of a whole and of a group.
- **[S]** Cambridge Stage 2 adds ¾.
- **Supported question types:**
  1. what fraction of a shape is shaded (½ or ¼)
  2. half or quarter of a group as a sharing story (mandazi, oranges)
  3. is it cut into equal parts? (yes/no)
  4. which picture shows a quarter?
  5. ¾ (Cambridge only)
- **Do not add:** ⅛, thirds, comparing fractions, or equivalence.

## G11_LIMITS: our current question has no syllabus line behind it
- **[S]** Cambridge 9709 P1: the gradient at a point is the limit of the
  gradients of a sequence of chords (chord from x = 2 to x = 2 + h on y = x³).
- **[K]** Only an informal idea of a limit is expected.
- **[S]** 0606: the idea of a derived function; no stated limits content.
- **[S]** KICD Grade 10: no limits.
- **[K]** Old KCSE: gradient from first principles.
- **Supported question types:**
  1. chord gradient 6 + h: what does it tend to as h → 0?
  2. lim (h → 0) [(4 + h)² − 16]/h
  3. the gradient of a chord for a given h (x = 1 to 1.1)
  4. factorising away a 0/0, only as a step inside 1-2
- **Not supported:** limits as x → ∞.
- **Note:** our current "lim(x→a) (bx + c)" is a warm-up at best.
