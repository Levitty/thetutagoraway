# Cambridge maths (Stages 1-9, IGCSE 0580): question types for HOREB

Research, 29-30 Sep 2026. **Source caveat:** page fetches to
cambridgeinternational.org and publisher PDFs were blocked from the research
environment, so every source is a search snippet. Per-stage objectives are
reconstructed from the 2020 frameworks (Primary 0096, Lower Secondary 0862) and
must be checked against the official PDFs before items are tagged by objective
code. Cambridge materials are copyrighted: everything here is paraphrased, with
invented numbers.

## Structure
- Primary 0096 (2020+): three strands: Number; Geometry and Measure; Statistics
  and Probability. Objective codes like `4Nf.02`; `1Nc.01` = count objects 0-20,
  estimate, read and write number names.
- Lower Secondary 0862 (2020+): Number; Algebra; Geometry and Measure;
  Statistics and Probability.
- "Five/six strands incl. problem solving" in some sources = the pre-2020
  frameworks (0845 / 1112). Ignore.
- Thinking and Working Mathematically (TWM), 4 pairs, woven into items:
  specialising/generalising, conjecturing/convincing,
  characterising/classifying, critiquing/improving.

## What a child must do, by stage (paraphrased, ±1 stage)
- **1:** count to 20 and estimate; bonds to 10; add/subtract within 20; double
  and halve; 2D/3D shapes; o'clock and half past; compare lengths; sort,
  pictograms.
- **2:** place value to 100; odd/even; 2-digit add/subtract; 2, 5, 10 tables;
  ½ and ¼; money; quarter past; cm, kg, litres on simple scales; symmetry;
  Carroll diagrams; chance words.
- **3:** place value to 1000; round to 10/100; column methods; 3, 4, 8 tables;
  unit and non-unit fractions; scales with unlabelled divisions; 5-minute
  times; right angles; perimeter; bar charts, Venn diagrams.
- **4:** to 10 000; negatives in context; all tables; 2-3 digit × 1 digit;
  remainders; tenths; equivalent fractions; 24-hour time, timetables; area by
  squares; compass, first-quadrant coordinates; reflection; frequency tables.
- **5:** hundredths; rounding decimals; multiples, factors, primes, squares;
  3-digit × 2-digit; compare and add fractions; percentages; ratio language;
  acute/obtuse/reflex and measuring; area of rectangles; line graphs;
  mode/median/range.
- **6 (Primary Checkpoint):** millions; negatives; order of operations; cubes;
  fraction × whole; FDP equivalence; % of amounts; ratio and direct proportion;
  angles in a triangle; radius/diameter; area of a triangle; transformations;
  four-quadrant coordinates; mean; pie charts; probability as a fraction.
- **7:** LCM/HCF, squares and roots; fractions and decimals, all operations; %
  increase/decrease; simplify, substitute; 1-2 step equations; sequences;
  angles on lines and parallels; compound area; cuboid volume; averages;
  stem-and-leaf; probability 0-1.
- **8:** indices; standard form basics; significant figures; brackets;
  unknowns on both sides; nth term; straight-line graphs; circles; bearings;
  enlargement; polygon angles; grouped data; scatter; relative frequency.
- **9 (Lower Secondary Checkpoint):** index laws (zero, negative); standard
  form; factorising; inequalities; simultaneous equations; Pythagoras; prisms
  and cylinders; loci and constructions; frequency polygons; sample spaces.

## Assessment format
- Primary Checkpoint: 2 papers × 40 marks × 45 min; Paper 1 non-calculator,
  Paper 2 calculator (moderate confidence).
- Lower Secondary Checkpoint: Paper 1 non-calculator, Paper 2 calculator, ~1 h.
- IGCSE 0580 (from 2025): Core Papers 1 (non-calc) and 3 (calc), 1h30, 80
  marks; Extended Papers 2 (non-calc) and 4 (calc), 2h, 100 marks.

## How Cambridge differs from rote practice
Items test relationships and reasoning: derived facts (use 23 × 14 = 322 to
find 2.3 × 14); balance equations (14 + □ = 20 − 3); always / sometimes /
never; critique a pupil's claim ("0.45 > 0.5 because 45 > 5"); odd one out;
estimate first; choose a method; "find two numbers with sum 11 and product
24". Examiners take method marks off for answers with no reasoning.
