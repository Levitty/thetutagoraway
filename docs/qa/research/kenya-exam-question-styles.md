# KPSEA / KJSEA / KEYA question styles for HOREB

Research, 30 Sep 2026. **Source caveat:** every Kenyan source (knec.ac.ke,
easyelimu, teacher.co.ke, scribd, news sites) was blocked from the research
environment; findings come from search snippets. [typical] = standard Kenyan
exam style from background knowledge, not checked against a fetched page.
All example wordings use invented numbers and names; no KNEC item is copied.
The most reliable check is a teacher login to cba.knec.ac.ke (sample papers).

## Exam formats
- **KPSEA (Grade 6):** all multiple choice, 30 maths items, options A-D,
  1 h 20 min, shaded circles on an OMR sheet from 2025. Reported out of 100.
  KNEC 2023 report: weak in fractions, geometry, measurement and applying
  skills to problems. (School-based vs national split: unconfirmed.)
- **KJSEA (Grade 9, first sat Oct-Nov 2025):** 2 h, 100 marks. Section A:
  20 MCQ (20 marks). Section B: 20 short structured items (80 marks), working
  shown, max 5 marks each. Placement weight: KJSEA 60%, Grades 7-8 SBA 20%,
  KPSEA 20%. Bands EE1 90-100, EE2 75-89, ME1 58-74, ME2 41-57, AE1 31-40,
  AE2 21-30, BE1 11-20, BE2 0-10. Strands: Numbers; Algebra; Measurements;
  Geometry; Data Handling and Probability.
- **KEYA (Grade 3):** teacher-marked in class, everyday situations: counting,
  operations, measurement, simple geometry, data, fractions, money, time.
- **Old KCPE:** 50 MCQ A-D, 2 h. Weak areas: ratio, percentages, averages,
  measurement.

## Kenyan contexts seen in items
- Money in "sh": shop price lists, paying with notes and finding the balance,
  profit on a school garden.
- Shamba split into crop portions; kales from a grocer; county deliveries.
- Matatu and bus journeys, timetables between towns, trips carrying guests.
- Agricultural show attendance, county population, bread factory, bookshop.
- Juice bottles in cartons (ml to litres), milk mixed with water, mm rings.
- Names from many communities: Nyaboke, Mugambi, Kioko, Halima, Wanyama, Asha.
- [typical] M-Pesa charges tables, harambee, school trips, KPLC and water
  bills, posho mill; coins sh 1, 5, 10, 20; notes sh 50-1000.

LP = Grades 1-3, UP = 4-6, JS = 7-9. MC = A-D; SA = short answer / working.

## Difficulty by grade
- **1-3:** whole numbers to about 1 000, one-step with pictures, coins and
  notes, clocks, shapes, tally charts.
- **4-6:** numbers to millions; one or two steps with sh, kg, litres, time.
  KPSEA items usually combine a conversion or "what's left" step with an
  arithmetic step, with four plausible options.
- **7-9:** integers, algebra, indices and logs, tables, Pythagoras, bearings,
  scale drawing, compound proportion, probability, hire purchase and
  interest. KJSEA mixes 1-mark MCQ with up to 5-mark structured items.
- **Implication:** Grades 4-6 practice should include A-D items with
  plausible distractors; Grades 7-9 need multi-step items with part marks.
