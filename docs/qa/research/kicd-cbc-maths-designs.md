## Grades 1-3
- **G1 [V]:** Number Concept (sorting, matching, ordering, patterns, number
  names); Whole Numbers; Measurement incl. time and Kenyan coins and notes.
  [P] patterns with beads and seeds; measuring with everyday objects;
  missing-number sentences.
- **G2 [V, earlier CBC guide]:** coins and notes to sh 100; counting in 1s,
  5s, 10s, 20s, 50s; same amount with different coins; money for goods and
  services. [P] place value with number tins and a chart.
- **G3 [V]:** fractions 1/2, 1/4, 1/8 by paper folding; money: change,
  balance, needs vs wants, features on Kenyan currency; multiplication × 1-10
  as repeated addition (fruit in groups at the market); division by repeated
  subtraction and sharing; analogue and digital clocks; estimate then measure
  up to 10 m.

## Grades 4-6
- **G4 [V]:** Numbers: place value and total value; numbers to 10 000;
  multiples to 100; factors to 50. [P] ×, ÷, fractions, decimals, length,
  area, mass, capacity, time, money, position and direction, angles, 2D
  shapes, data. Roman numerals: secondary evidence only.
- **G5 [V]:** Numbers, Measurement, Geometry, Data Handling. [P] budget;
  grams, dividing g/kg; horizontal and vertical lines; angles with a unit
  angle then a protractor; 3D objects in the school compound.
- **G6 [V, near-verbatim]:** place value and total value to millions; numbers
  to millions in symbols; read and write to 100 000 in words; order to
  100 000; round to the nearest thousand; squares of whole numbers to 100.
  [P] a.m./p.m., 12 ↔ 24-hour, travel timetables (Nairobi-Nakuru bus).

## Grades 7-9
- **G7 [V]:**
  - Numbers: whole numbers, factors, fractions, decimals, squares and roots
  - Algebra: expressions, linear equations, linear inequalities
  - Measurements: Pythagorean relationship, length, area, volume and
    capacity, time distance and speed, temperature, money
  - Geometry: angles, constructions
  - Data: frequency tables, pictographs, bar, pie, line graphs
  - [P] money: profit, loss, % profit, % discount, price lists, e-money
    tariff charts, dummy cheques. Postal charges and mobile money appeared
    pre-2024 and may be gone.
- **G8 [V strands; P content]:** integers; recurring decimals; squares and
  roots from tables; ratio, direct and inverse proportion; circumference and
  arcs; compound interest to 3 years; hire purchase; scale drawing;
  coordinates and graphs (tables of values); common solids.
- **G9 [V]:**
  - Numbers: integers, cubes and cube roots, indices and logarithms,
    compound proportion and rates of work
  - Algebra: matrices, equations of straight lines, linear inequalities
  - Measurements: area, volume, mass/volume/density, time distance speed,
    money (currency conversion, excise duty, VAT), approximation and errors
  - Geometry: coordinates and graphs, scale drawing, similarity and
    enlargement, trigonometry
  - Data handling and probability

## Grade 10 (senior school)
Two courses, both with strands Numbers and Algebra; Measurements and Geometry;
Statistics and Probability.
- **Core [V]:** real numbers, indices and logarithms, quadratics; similarity
  and enlargement, reflection and congruence, rotation, trigonometry I, area
  of polygons, circles, surface area and volume, vectors I, linear motion;
  statistics I, probability I.
- **Essential [V]:** 13 sub-strands (3 / 8 / 2) starting with real numbers;
  other names not found. 5 × 40-min lessons a week.
- Grades 11-12: nothing found.

## Assessment language
- KICD rubric: Exceeds / Meets / Approaches / Below Expectation (EE, ME, AE,
  BE) per sub-strand; descriptor wording not seen.
- KJSEA 8-level scale: EE1 90-100 (8 pts), EE2 75-89 (7), ME1 58-74 (6),
  ME2 41-57 (5), AE1 31-40 (4), AE2 21-30 (3), BE1 11-20 (2), BE2 1-10 (1).
  (the-star.co.ke, 15 Dec 2025)

## Easy-to-miss topics to check against HOREB
- Kenyan coins and notes, change, needs vs wants (G1-3)
- Roman numerals (G4, unconfirmed)
- Squares to 100 (G6); roots (G7); roots from tables (G8)
- 12/24-hour time and timetables (G6)
- Budget (G5)
- Profit, loss, discount, price lists, e-money tariffs, cheques (G7)
- Compound interest, hire purchase (G8)
- Currency conversion, excise duty, VAT (G9)
- Cubes and cube roots, logs, rates of work, matrices, approximation and
  errors, density (G9)
- Temperature, Pythagorean relationship (G7)
- Not found in CBC: number bases, KRA income tax, utility bills

## Kenyan contexts named
Imitation money and a classroom shop, the local market, number tins, beads
and seeds, paper folding, measuring the classroom and compound, price lists,
e-money tariff charts, dummy cheques, Nairobi-Nakuru bus timetables.
