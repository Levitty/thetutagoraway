## Keep, with a teacher note

All other families were true and at level. Two general points. First, in several "why" families (for example the widow's son, the lost coin) a wrong option is tied to a misconception code that does not match it ("Do nothing, because it is not her child" carries the Jairus line), so the child reads a true line that is off the point; nothing false is said, but the lines read oddly. Second, a few options are nonsense distractors ("Only the busy", "To be old with many degrees"); they do no harm. Points for a CRE teacher are in `TEACHER_QUESTIONS_13NOV.md`: Childline 116, the child-protection items, "born of water and the Spirit", "bought at a price", the wording of the Lord's Prayer, repent and be baptised, the birds in the mustard tree and the coin as a lost person, the groups that reject Jesus, the Balaam wording, and the drugs and harmful-video items.
