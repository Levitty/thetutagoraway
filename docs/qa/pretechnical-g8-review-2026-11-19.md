### pretechnical8.fc.direct

Skill `FOUNDA.FIRE_SAFETY.FIRE_CAUSES`, outcome `a`, form direct, rung 1.

**Q:** Why must paraffin be kept away from a stove?
Options: It only spoils the food that is cooking | It is heavy and may fall on the floor | It will turn into water when it gets warm | **Its vapour can catch fire easily**
- If a child answers "It only spoils the food that is cooking": Most fires start from something people did or forgot to do. Fuels burn easily, so they are stored away from heat.
- If a child answers "It is heavy and may fall on the floor": Most fires start from something people did or forgot to do. Most fires at home start from careless human acts such as an unattended candle or stove.

### pretechnical8.fc.story

Skill `FOUNDA.FIRE_SAFETY.FIRE_CAUSES`, outcome `a`, form word, rung 2.

**Q:** A farmer burns rubbish and leaves while it still burns in dry weather. What is the danger?
Options: The smoke will make the grass grow faster | Fires only start in the farm from lightning | **Sparks can spread to dry grass and start a fire** | The rubbish will turn to ash at once
- If a child answers "The smoke will make the grass grow faster": Most fires start from something people did or forgot to do. Overloaded sockets and damaged wires get hot and can start a fire.
- If a child answers "Fires only start in the farm from lightning": Most fires start from something people did or forgot to do. Fuels burn easily, so they are stored away from heat.

### pretechnical8.fp.direct

Skill `FOUNDA.FIRE_SAFETY.FIRE_PREVENT`, outcome `b`, form direct, rung 1.

**Q:** Which action helps people escape from a fire?
